# Working rules for this project

These apply to anyone working here, people and AI assistants alike. Edit them for your project; keep them short.

1. **Clean room.** Nothing from any past employer: no screens, data, internal terms, metrics or workflows.
2. **Label simulated work.** Every simulated persona, interview, quote or test is marked SIMULATED, until real research replaces it.
3. **Sources for every claim.** Research and competitor claims link to the page they came from. "Not mentioned" is not the same as "no".
4. **Log prompts.** Each working session gets a file in `prompts/` with each prompt, what came back, and the call made (`templates/prompt-log.md`).
5. **Record decisions.** One short file per decision in `decisions/`, listed in `decisions/README.md` (`templates/decision.md`).
6. **Updates log.** Every commit adds one line to `updates.md`, in the same commit.
7. **A person decides.** AI drafts; nothing ships, saves or sends without a person reading it first.
8. **Accessibility.** WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, a visible focus state, labels for screen readers, and reduced motion respected.
9. **Phones too.** No page scrolls sideways at 375 or 320 pixels.
10. **Plain copy.** Short sentences, no lines ending in one word, no boastful labels.
11. **Checks before done.** Type check, lint and tests pass, and `checks/` finds nothing.
