# Stage 2 · Prove it isn't built already

> Before we design anything, try to prove the idea already exists. Search for products that [the core things the product does]. Read each product's own pages and cite them; mark anything from a third party, and say "not mentioned" rather than "no".
> Tell me which come closest and what is still open. Then turn every product into one visual for a single slide, readable in five seconds.

**Your checkpoint:** if the first scan says nobody does it, don't trust it yet: ask for a wider search. Record where the real gap is.
